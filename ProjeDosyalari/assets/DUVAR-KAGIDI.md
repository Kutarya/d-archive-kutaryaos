# Duvar kâğıdı kaynağı

Araç: yerleşik ImageGen. Üretilen raster: kutarya-dag-golu.png.
Masaüstü çekimi değildir; uygulamanın arka plan varlığıdır. Üç renk görünümü
uygulamada GDI+ ColorMatrix ile uygulanır. İkonlar mevcut C# çizim sistemidir.

Kullanılan istem:

Create a refined original desktop wallpaper for Kutarya OS, a C# Windows Forms
student operating-system simulation. Landscape 16:9, ideally 1920x1080. A serene
Anatolian mountain lake at early dawn, layered blue-green ridges fading into
light mist, warm peach sunlight just touching the distant peaks, tiny pine
silhouettes on the lower-right shore, detailed calm water with subtle reflections,
natural fine rock and forest texture. Elegant photographic illustration,
restrained cinematic light, rich but muted teal/navy and small amber accents,
no oversaturation. The left 30 percent must be calm and slightly darker for white
desktop icon labels, the center must remain clean and airy for app windows,
visual detail on the far right and bottom. This is a functional desktop
background, not a UI mockup. Absolutely no text, letters, logos, watermark,
icons, buttons, frame, people or houses.
